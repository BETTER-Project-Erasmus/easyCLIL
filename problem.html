CLIL is easy!

Solve a problem

At the start of the lesson, the teacher presents a problem to the pupils, who must solve it. At the end of the lesson, the pupils must explain their solution whilst the teacher helps them to improve their language skills.
Step 1 - Presentation of the problem
Step 2 - Solve the problem
Step 3 - Explain and learn
Step 4 - Validation of the new knowledge

The teacher prepares an explanation using flashcards, a poster or a text to introduce the new content. The aim is to devote time to teaching the linguistic elements and knowledge and concepts related to the topic.
